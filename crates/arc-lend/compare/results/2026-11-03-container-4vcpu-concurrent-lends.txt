## 8. Concurrent lends on one cell

Each thread lends flat out from a reader of its own, with no publisher, for 1000 ms, and the table gives the mean time per lend across threads. A `Reader` is 16 bytes. *adjacent*: the readers sit next to each other in one `Vec<Reader>`, so a lend that wrote anywhere in the `Reader` would make the threads contend for a cache line. *apart*: each reader has a cache line to itself, whatever a lend writes. A lend writes only its slot, which has a line of its own, so the two columns should agree.

| threads | adjacent | apart |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 10.19 ns | 11.61 ns |
| 2 | 13.45 ns | 13.41 ns |
| 4 | 26.28 ns | 25.89 ns |
